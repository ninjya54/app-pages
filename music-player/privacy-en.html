Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026　/　日本語

This is an English translation provided for convenience. If there is any difference between this translation and the Japanese version, the Japanese version prevails.

1. Information the App accesses

Sonic Wave Player respects your privacy. The App accesses the following, but the developer does not directly collect or transmit personal information:

  • Music files on your device (for playback only)
  • Audio session information (for the visualizer's frequency analysis; audio is not recorded)
  • Custom background images (stored on the device, never sent externally)

2. How information is used

The information accessed is used only to provide the App's features. The developer does not directly share your personal information with third parties.

3. Data storage

Favorites, playlists, equalizer settings, background images, and similar data are stored only on your device (SharedPreferences and internal storage).

4. Third-party services

The App uses the following third-party services. Each processes data under its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob: To show ads. Your advertising ID and device information may be sent to Google. Ads are hidden for Premium members. In regions where consent is required, such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the App shows a screen at first launch asking for your consent to the use of information for ads (using Google's consent management). You can change or withdraw your choice anytime in the App under "Settings" > "Ad privacy settings".
    Google Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat: To manage subscriptions. Purchase information is processed on RevenueCat's servers.
    RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Billing: To process in-app purchases.

5. Permissions

The permissions the App requests and why:

  • READ_MEDIA_AUDIO (Android 13+) / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 12 and earlier): To read music files
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE: To keep playing in the background
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+): To show the playback control notification
  • MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS: For the equalizer and visualizer
  • RECORD_AUDIO: Android requires this permission for the visualizer to obtain the waveform and frequencies of the audio the App is playing. The App does not record from the microphone, and does not store or transmit audio. Music playback works without this permission (the visualizer is not shown).
  • WAKE_LOCK: To keep playing while the screen is off
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID): To show ads. Ads are not shown to Premium members.
  • INTERNET: For ads and subscription management

6. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes are posted on this page.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, please use the Support page. For the conditions of use of the App, see the Terms of Use.
